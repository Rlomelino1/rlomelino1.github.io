Frequently asked questions

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Every puzzle is generated on your device, and the whole game works offline, including the daily puzzle.

I bought Remove Ads. How do I get it back on a new device?

Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in to the same Google Play or Apple account you bought it with. Your purchase stays active offline too.

Where is my progress saved? Can I move it to another phone?

Progress, stats, streaks and achievements are saved only on your device. There are no accounts, so progress does not transfer between devices, and uninstalling the app deletes it.

How do I start over?

Settings → Reset all data clears puzzles, stats and your streak. It does not remove a Remove Ads purchase.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

If you are in a region that requires consent, Settings shows Ad Privacy Choices, where you can change your answer at any time. On iOS you can also turn off tracking in the system Settings app under Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Is every puzzle solvable?

Yes. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, and you can reach it by logic alone, without guessing.
