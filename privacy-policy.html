Privacy Policy

Offline Nonogram · Effective September 30, 2026

Offline Nonogram is a puzzle game made by Rlomelino1. It is built to work without an internet connection. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Information stored on your device

Your puzzles in progress, solved puzzles, stats, streaks, achievements, unlocked themes and settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or to any server we run. There are no accounts and no sign-in.

You can delete this data at any time with Settings → Reset all data, or by uninstalling the app.

Advertising

When your device is online, the free version shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • Your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA)
  • Device information such as model, operating system and language
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Ad interactions, such as whether an ad was shown or tapped

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice anytime from Settings → Ad privacy choices. On iOS, the app asks permission before using your advertising ID, and ads are not personalized if you decline.

When the device is offline, no ads are requested and nothing is sent. Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases

You can buy "Remove ads" as a one-time purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store, and we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to verify the purchase and restore it on your devices. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and the purchase receipt from the store. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No crash reporting services
  • No selling or sharing of personal information beyond the ad and purchase services above

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
