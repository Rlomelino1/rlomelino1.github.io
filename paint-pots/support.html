Support

Paint Pots: Color Sort · iPhone and Android

Found a bug, have a question, or want to suggest something? Email PaintPotsColorSort@proton.me. Please include your phone model and the level you are on. Every message is read, and replies usually come within a few days.

In the game, Settings → Support → Contact support starts the email for you with the game's version, your phone model and your level already filled in.

Common questions

How do I play?

Tap a jar to pick it up, then tap another jar to pour. Paint only pours onto the same color or into an empty jar. Fill every jar with a single color to finish the level. Each level you finish paints a piece of a picture.

What do Undo and Extra jar do?

Undo takes back your last pour. Extra jar adds an empty jar for this level. You get a few free ones, and you can earn more with coins.

Is every level solvable?

Yes. Every level is checked before you see it, so there is always a way to finish without luck.

What is the daily painting?

A small new painting every day, the same one for every player. It changes at 04:00 UTC. Finish it on days in a row to build your streak.

Why don't I see my score on the leaderboard?

Leaderboards use Game Center on iPhone and Google Play Games on Android. Make sure you are signed in to one of them in your phone's settings. If you played offline, your score is sent the next time the game is online.

I bought Remove ads but still see ads

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the game?

Your progress is saved only on your phone, so deleting the game deletes it too. Your Remove ads purchase can always be restored.

How do I start over?

Settings → Erase all progress clears your levels, paintings, coins and streak. It keeps your Remove ads purchase.
