Privacy Policy

Paint Pots: Color Sort · Effective November 19, 2026

Paint Pots: Color Sort ("Paint Pots") is a color sorting puzzle game made by an independent developer. You can play it without an internet connection and without an account. This policy explains what information the game handles and why.

Information stored on your device

Your level progress, finished paintings, collection, coins, daily paintings, streak and settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or to any server we run. There are no accounts and no sign-in to Paint Pots itself.

You can delete this data at any time with Settings → Erase all progress, or by uninstalling the game.

Leaderboards

If you are signed in to Game Center (iPhone and iPad) or Google Play Games (Android), the game sends your daily painting score (the number of moves) and the number of levels you have finished to Apple or Google, so they can show the leaderboards. Your scores appear there with your Game Center or Play Games name. Apple and Google handle this information under their own privacy policies: Apple Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

If you are not signed in, nothing is sent and the game works the same. You can sign out of Game Center or Play Games in your device's settings at any time.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily painting reminder, it is scheduled on your device. No information is sent to us or anyone else to deliver it. You can turn it off in Settings or in your device's notification settings.

Advertising

When your device is online, the free version shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • Your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA)
  • Device information such as model, operating system and language
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Ad interactions, such as whether an ad was shown or tapped

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice anytime from Settings → Ad privacy choices. On iOS, the game asks permission before using your advertising ID, and ads are not personalized if you decline.

When your device is offline, no ads are requested. Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases

You can buy "Remove ads" as a one-time purchase. Payment is handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play, and we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to verify the purchase and restore it on your devices. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and the purchase receipt from the store. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Game updates

When your device is online, the game may check for and download small updates through Expo, the service used to build it. This check includes basic technical information such as the game's version, your device's platform and your IP address, and it is used only to deliver the right update. See Expo's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No crash reporting services
  • No selling or sharing of personal information beyond the services described above

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
